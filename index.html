Overview

The workshop AGENT Forum 2025 will be held on Wednesday 9th June – Friday 11th May 2025 at the Aalto University.

The abbreviation AGENT stands for Analysis, Geometry, and Number Theory. The areas should be interpreted broadly. The workshop is mainly aimed at young researchers (PhD students, postdocs) working in Finland on these areas of mathematics. Our main aim is to enable networking across universities, and across different fields of research. Everyone is welcome to participate, and we encourage participation by people of all career stages.

The first edition of the conference took place in 2021 at Aalto University (http://math.aalto.fi/conferences/agent2021/). The conference was last organized at University of Jyväskylä in 2024.
